1. **DIPLOMACY / ASIA — Trump, Xi and Putin hold first-ever trilateral summit, sign 'peace declaration'.**

   first trilateral discussion; Xi called it an event of "groundbreaking
   significance" and the trio released a joint declaration of peace — but AP

   Sources: AP ("Trump, Xi and Putin hold rare 3-way talks on nuclear risks

   BBC ("Trump, Putin and Xi release 'peace declaration' after historic
   meeting"; "This is a remarkable diplomatic moment for China's Xi — but it

2. **ECONOMY / NORTH AMERICA — Anthropic lists in the biggest IPO on record.**
   The AI company began trading at a $1.5 trillion valuation in "the biggest
   IPO of all time," the first purely AI company to go public; the stock
   closed up 17% on day one, though the wider market ignored it — US stocks
   tumbled 2% toward their longest losing streak since 2024. Dario Amodei
   pledged at the listing that AI systems will "remain under human control."
   Sources: AP ("Anthropic begins trading in the biggest IPO on record
   despite the many fears surrounding AI"; "Anthropic to debut on Wall
   Street with a $1.5 trillion valuation in the biggest IPO of all time");
   BBC ("Anthropic becomes one of world's most valuable public companies in
   stock market debut"; "shares closed up 17%").

3. **JUSTICE / ASIA — UN's top court finds a state guilty of genocide for the first time: Myanmar.**
   The International Court of Justice ruled that Myanmar committed genocide
   against the Rohingya minority in the 2017 military crackdown that killed
   thousands — the first time any state has been found responsible for
   genocide by an international court.
   Sources: AP ("For the first time, UN's highest court finds a country
   responsible for genocide: Myanmar"); BBC ("Myanmar committed genocide
   against Rohingya minority, top UN court rules").
